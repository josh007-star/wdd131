Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Nigerian Football Hub

⚽ NIGERIAN FOOTBALL HUB

I chose the name Nigerian Football Hub because the website will be a central place where football fans can learn about Nigerian football. The name represents the passion Nigerians have for the game and makes it clear what visitors will find on the website.

Proposed domain: nigerianfootballhub.com

2. Site Purpose

Nigerian Football Hub is a website created to inform, educate, and entertain people who are interested in Nigerian football. It will provide information about the Super Eagles, popular Nigerian football players, the history of Nigerian football, and the achievements Nigeria has recorded in local and international competitions.

The website will contain three main pages: Home, Players, and Achievements. The Home page will introduce visitors to Nigerian football and its history. The Players page will showcase famous Nigerian footballers and their careers. The Achievements page will highlight important trophies, records, and memorable moments.

Visitors will also be able to use a form to share their favourite Nigerian football player.

3. Scenarios

These are questions that visitors to the website may ask:

  • Who are some of the most famous Nigerian football players and what have they achieved in their careers?
  • What major trophies and achievements have the Super Eagles won?
  • What is the history of the Nigerian national football team?
  • How can I share my favourite Nigerian football player on the website?

4. Color Schema

The website will use colors inspired by the Nigerian national flag. Green represents Nigeria and its pride, white represents peace, and dark green provides a strong contrast for important sections.

Nigerian Football Hub will celebrate Nigerian football by providing useful information about the national team, famous players, football history, and achievements. The website will use a responsive design so that visitors can access it easily on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
